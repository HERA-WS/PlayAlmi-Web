Chapter I The World of Valdgrímr

In the northern reaches of feudal Europe, nestled among the frost-bitten fjords and iron-grey skies, lay the forgotten kingdom of Valdgrímr. Spanning the eleventh through fourteenth centuries, this remote Norse realm was home to hardy warriors, devout monks, and ancient superstitions older than the stones of its castles.

Valdgrímr was not a prosperous land. Winters were long, harvests uncertain, and the sea brought more grief than gold. Yet its people endured — until the night the dead refused to rest.

"They came at dusk. Not from the forest, not from the hills — but from the earth itself."

What began as whispered rumours became an unstoppable tide. A virulent and unnatural plague swept across Valdgrímr, corrupting the living and raising the recently buried. The infected retained a ghastly mockery of their former martial ability, making them far more dangerous than mindless shambling creatures.

Church scholars and court mages pointed their trembling fingers at a single source: a sorcerer of immense dark power known only as The Pale Architect — a being who had seeded the plague deliberately, for purposes no living soul yet understood.

Chapter II The Story So Far
👑
The King's Commission
King Harald of Valdgrímr, watching his realm crumble from behind the barred gates of his keep, made one desperate decision: send his finest knight into the heart of the infestation.
⚔️
Sir Ocelot Volunteers
Sir Ocelot — tournament champion, veteran of three border wars, and the only knight who volunteered — was given a simple, terrible commission: ride to the three zones of greatest undead concentration, destroy whatever commands the dead there, and return with intelligence about The Pale Architect. He was not expected to survive.
🛡️
Into the Grey
"I do not ride to win glory, Your Majesty. I ride because someone must." Armed with sword, shield, and the relics his chaplain pressed into his hands at the city gate, Sir Ocelot rode north into the grey.

Chapter IV Enemies of the Dead
⚠️The Undead of Valdgrímr are not mindless corpses. The plague preserves whatever martial skill the host possessed in life — making former soldiers, knights, and warriors the most lethal specimens. Expect relentless attacks, and know that enemies fall only when struck with decisive force.
Enemy Difficulty HP Behavior Points
⚔️ Undead Knights Common · All Levels 3 Pursue & melee — relentless 10
💀Once the knights of Valdgrímr's lesser houses, these warriors fell to the plague early and in great numbers. They retain their battle instincts — shield raised, sword arm swinging — and will press forward without fear or hesitation. They are the foot soldiers of the dead, and the bulk of every wave you will face.
💡Damage: 1 per strike. Each Undead Knight killed awards 10 points.
Chapter V The Bosses
⚠️Each level is presided over by a unique and devastatingly powerful undead commander. They are faster, more resilient, and far more aggressive than any common foe. Learn their patterns — it is your only advantage.
🩸 GORGATH THE EVISCERATOR
Boss — Level 1 · Boss (Phase II) — Level 3
Hit Points: 20
Damage: 2 per strike
Points: 50
Attack Range: Extended

A former plague-doctor turned plague-weapon, Gorgath was among the first to fall and the first to rise again. His massive frame and iron mace make him a fearsome threat at close quarters. In Level 1 he leads the final wave — in Level 3, he returns in a more powerful form before the true final confrontation.

⚡Phase II (Level 3): The plague has continued to transform him. What you face is a creature that has shed the last vestiges of its humanity — faster, more aggressive, and enraged.
🦴 LORD MARROW
Boss — Level 2
Hit Points: 20
Damage: 2 per strike
Points: 50
Attack Style: Aggressive charge

Lord Marrow was once a minor baron of Valdgrímr's eastern marches — a brutal man even in life. Death and the plague have stripped away whatever restraint he once had, leaving only a relentless, lurching killing machine that wields its longsword like a battering ram.

💀 THE PALE ARCHITECT
Final Boss — Level 3 · ??? (Sprite kept secret)

Beyond Gorgath's second form lies the architect of all your suffering. The Pale Architect is the sorcerer who unleashed the plague upon Valdgrímr, and he awaits Sir Ocelot at the heart of Level 3 — crown upon his head, staff in hand, and the dead answering his every gesture.

His true name, his origin, and the full extent of his power remain hidden until confronted. What the Church's records do confirm: he has orchestrated undead uprisings before, in other kingdoms, under other names. Valdgrímr is not his first experiment.

💬"All flesh returns to the earth. I merely… accelerate the process."

Chapter VII Abilities & Power-Ups
📦When an Undead Knight is slain, it may drop one of four sacred relics. Walk over a dropped relic to collect it instantly. Bosses do not drop abilities. In later waves the drop rate increases — keep your eyes on the ground.
📖
BIBLE
Destroys every low-rank undead in the arena. Bosses are immune.
🛡️
SHIELD
Complete damage immunity for a short time.
❤️
HEART
Restores one lost life. Cannot exceed maximum lives.
✝️
CRUCIFIX
Doubles attack damage for a limited time.
⚠️The Bible and the Shield are rarer drops than the Heart and the Crucifix. Do not count on them appearing predictably.

Chapter X Gameplay
🎮Age of the Dead is a top-down arena combat game. Each level consists of multiple waves of enemies that spawn at the edges of the map. Your objective is simple: survive every wave and defeat the boss.
🌊 The Wave System
›Enemies spawn in set numbers and immediately seek Sir Ocelot.
›The next wave begins only when every enemy is defeated.
›Each wave brings more enemies and shorter gaps.
›The boss appears in the final wave. Level ends only when the boss is slain.
❤️ Lives & Damage
›Starts each level with three lives shown as hearts.
›Each hit removes one heart. Brief invincibility follows.
›Lose all hearts → Defeat screen.
🏆 Scoring & Timer
›Undead Knights = 10 pts. Bosses = 50 pts.
›A live timer tracks your run duration.
›Score and time displayed on Victory/Defeat screen.
